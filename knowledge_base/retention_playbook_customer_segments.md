# Customer Segment Guidance

## Newer customers
Customers with short tenure need reassurance that they made the right choice. Early-life retention should emphasize onboarding, usage education, and quick issue resolution rather than only pricing.

## Senior citizens and dependents
Senior-citizen households or customers supporting dependents may respond better to simpler plans, trust-building communication, and predictable billing than to aggressive upsell campaigns.

## Stable no-churn customers
If the model predicts no churn, do not overreact with discounts. Instead, watch the few weak signals, reinforce value, and focus on preventive steps that keep the account healthy.
