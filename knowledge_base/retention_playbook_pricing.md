# Pricing and Contract Retention

## Month-to-month customers
Customers on month-to-month plans are more likely to churn because they face lower switching friction. When the account is already high risk, the first retention action should be an annual-plan migration offer with a price lock or short introductory discount.

## High monthly charges
Customers with high monthly charges react strongly to billing pressure. If monthly charges are elevated, recommend a bill review, a targeted discount, or a bundle simplification before offering broader service changes.

## Total charges vs tenure
When total charges are already high but tenure is still low, the customer may feel they have paid a lot without building loyalty. In those cases, onboarding value and pricing reassurance matter more than generic marketing messages.
