# Billing and Payment Behavior

## Electronic check risk
Electronic check users often show higher churn than customers on automated payment methods. Good retention actions include moving the customer to bank transfer or credit-card autopay with a small incentive.

## Paperless billing concerns
Paperless billing is convenient for many users, but some customers still need better billing visibility. If a customer is sensitive to charges, pair paperless billing with a clearer invoice explanation or a guided billing walkthrough.

## Reduce friction
Billing friction is a strong churn trigger. Any payment flow with repeated confusion, manual effort, or failed expectations should be treated as a retention priority even when the base model risk is only medium.
