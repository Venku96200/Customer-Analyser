# Add-on and Support Retention

## Missing tech support
Customers without tech support are more likely to leave when they hit service issues. A free trial of premium support or a dedicated support callback can reduce churn pressure for vulnerable accounts.

## Missing online security or backup
When online security, backup, or device protection are missing, the service can feel less sticky. Offer feature bundles that increase perceived value rather than only discounting the base plan.

## Fiber customers with support gaps
Fiber customers can be profitable but sensitive to service-quality expectations. If a fiber customer also lacks support add-ons, prioritize service assurance and a stronger support package.
